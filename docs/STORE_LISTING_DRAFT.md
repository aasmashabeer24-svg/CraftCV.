## Full description
Bring your career story to life with CraftCV — a creative resume studio.

Make a professional CV with flexible templates or create your own style. Adjust fonts, colors, margins, headings, portraits and section order with an interactive live preview. Create multiple resume versions, get writing suggestions, and export your work.

Features:
- Resume templates for creative, professional and ATS-conscious styles.
- Build Your Own Design with custom colors, layouts and typography.
- Add work experience, education, projects, skills and accomplishments.
- Preview your resume as you edit.
- Save resumes on your device or sign in for optional cloud synchronization.
- AI-assisted writing and job-description comparison when service is available.
- Word export and PDF printing (verify Android workflows before publishing).

CraftCV needs an internet connection for online AI and cloud features. Features may require a free account, and availability depends on the connected service.
