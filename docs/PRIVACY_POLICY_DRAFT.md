## Information stored on your device

The native offline CV builder stores the information you enter in app-private storage on your Android device. This can include your name, email address, phone number, location, professional summary, work history, education, skills, projects, certifications, languages, awards, interests and optional profile photo. Your PDF and Word exports are saved only when you choose a destination through Android's document picker.

Native offline CVs are not automatically uploaded or synchronized to CraftCV Online Studio. Removing the app or deleting a local CV may remove the local copy; keep your own export or backup if you need one.

## Optional Cloud Account

If you choose to sign in to CraftCV Online Studio and upload a CV, the service receives the account identity supplied by the authentication provider and the CV data you select to upload. A CV may contain contact details, work and education history, skills, projects, links and an optional profile photo. Cloud CVs are stored in an account-scoped data table and are accessed over HTTPS. Cloud CVs can be deleted individually from the account.

**Account deletion is not yet implemented for the current authentication integration.** Do not publish this draft or submit a Data Safety declaration that claims account deletion is available until a complete in-app and web account-deletion process has been implemented and verified. The authentication SDK currently exposes sign-in, session restoration and sign-out, but not account deletion.

## Optional AI writing assistance

AI assistance is an online feature and requires an internet connection. When you explicitly select an AI action and press Generate, CraftCV sends only the selected target role, professional summary, skills, work-experience notes, project details and, when relevant, the job description you supplied. The current AI request does not include your name, email, phone number, location or profile photo.

The AI service processes this text to return writing suggestions. Review every suggestion for accuracy. AI output is not applied to your CV until you choose to apply it. Do not submit confidential information or information you do not have permission to share.

## Advertising and consent

The Android project includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Debug builds use Google's official test ad identifiers. Production ads are disabled unless the developer configures valid production AdMob identifiers for a release build. If production ads are enabled, the final policy and Google Play Data Safety answers must be updated to reflect the actual SDK data collection, processing and sharing practices, including consent requirements and the applicable Google disclosures.

## Data security and retention

Online requests use HTTPS. Native offline CV content is kept in app-private storage. Cloud CVs remain stored until the user deletes them individually or a verified deletion process is completed. The developer must define a retention period for backups, service logs and deletion requests before publication.

## Your choices

- You can edit or delete local CVs within the native app.
- You can choose whether to use Cloud Account features.
- You can delete individual cloud CVs from the online account.
- You can choose whether to send CV text to AI by not using the AI feature.
- You can choose whether to add a profile photo; it is optional.
