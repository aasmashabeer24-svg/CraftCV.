## Full description draft

Create a clear, professional CV on your Android phone with CraftCV.

CraftCV is an offline-first resume builder for students, fresh graduates and experienced professionals. Build your CV step by step, keep multiple drafts on your device, choose a clean design and export a document you can share with employers.

**CV builder**
- Guided four-step resume creation.
- Save and edit multiple CVs on your phone.
- Add personal details, professional summary, work experience and projects.
- Add multiple education records, including school, college, university, diploma and professional qualifications.
- Organize skills into categories such as Technical Skills, Soft Skills, Tools & Software, Business Skills, Creative Skills and Languages.
- Add certifications, achievements, languages and other relevant details.

**Professional documents**
- Choose from 12 native CV styles.
- Preview your finished A4 resume.
- Export and share PDF files.
- Export editable Word DOCX files.
- Create a cover letter.

**Offline-first**
- Create and edit your CV without creating an account.
- Your native offline CVs stay in the app's private storage on this device.
- Online features, when enabled, require an internet connection and are separate from local offline CV storage.

Review every detail before sending your CV. AI-generated suggestions, if enabled in the final release, may contain mistakes and should be checked for accuracy. CraftCV does not guarantee ATS rankings, interviews or employment.

## Claims to avoid

- “Guaranteed ATS pass”, “100% ATS score”, “guaranteed job” or “get hired instantly”.
- Claims that native offline CVs sync automatically to the cloud.
- Claims that AI suggestions are always accurate.
